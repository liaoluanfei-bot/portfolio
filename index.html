UI/UX

Baidu App - AI Publisher

Background

Baidu App is China’s largest search and information platform, connecting hundreds of millions of users to content and knowledge every day. content creators use the “Publisher” to create and distribute their works, allowing quality content to be discovered and shared with a wider audience.

Challenges

Today’s content platforms are more competitive than ever. Creators don’t lack the will to express—they struggle with complex tools, steep learning curves, and fading inspiration. How might Baidu App’s Publisher help ordinary users express themselves effortlessly?

Strategy

We created a conversational experience that guides inspiration and supports AI-assisted, multimodal generation — enabling users to produce rich, dynamic content with just a single sentence.

My Role

I was independently responsible for the UI design of the project and contributed to the UX proposal that was later adopted.

01

Inspiration-driven, one-click content generation

After entering the AI creation flow, the system inspires users through diverse creative inspirations and smart presets. With one click, users can quickly generate content, enhancing both creative efficiency and the overall experience.

02

Creative inspirations

After users click on an inspiration card, the system provides popular AI features such as visual effects and trending topics. By generating images, videos, and text through AI, it helps users quickly complete their posts.

03

Smart Presets

By selecting or writing prompts, users can instantly experience a conversational AI content generation process that guides them from inspiration to creation. The system responds in real time, generating multimodal outputs such as text, images, or videos based on the user’s intent.

04

Card Design

In the AI creation process, the card serves as the core carrier of information and interaction. It integrates multiple states — including task execution, content generation, and user feedback — within a single modular component. Through componentization and layered nesting, the card adapts flexibly to various content types (images, videos, and text), ensuring visual consistency while improving both design and development efficiency.

05

Staged Task Progress

The task process is displayed in stages, with each step collapsing automatically once completed — easing users’ anxiety while waiting and making the generation progress more transparent.

06

Instant Result Optimization

After task planning is completed, the AI generates the corresponding results. To make it easier for users to fine-tune the results, the system provides smart edit suggestions based on the generated output. With one click, users can quickly regenerate an optimized version.

07

One-click Publishing

By clicking “Publish”, the system automatically handles content distribution and presentation in the background, enabling a seamless transition from creation to sharing while significantly improving publishing efficiency and overall user experience.
